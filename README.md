# SecureTheCloud AI Governance Board

**Status:** Lab Platform / Phase 0 Baseline

SecureTheCloud AI Governance Board is a simulated enterprise AI governance review platform for AI system intake, model registry, risk classification, review workflow, regulatory mapping, approval evidence, and executive oversight.

This lab demonstrates how organizations can govern AI systems before adoption, deployment, sensitive-data access, or production use.

## Core Principle

AI systems may be proposed, reviewed, risk-scored, mapped, approved, monitored, and governed.

AI systems may not be deployed, connected to sensitive data, used with PHI, classified as low risk, or promoted to production without governance review, risk mapping, approval evidence, and ongoing oversight.

## Lab Boundary

This is a simulated, production-shaped lab.

It does not provide production authorization, legal compliance advice, SOC 2 certification, HIPAA compliance certification, EU AI Act compliance certification, real clinical decision support, real regulated-system access, real patient data processing, or production enforcement authority.
